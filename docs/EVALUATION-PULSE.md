Mean pairwise distance: planted 0.300, estimated 0.244; without the one-day spike 0.257; regenerated without replays (repeat probability 0) and without the spike 0.299. The estimator therefore recovers the planted preferences when listening follows preferences; the smaller estimated distances come from the simulated behaviour — replays pick uniformly among a user's previously heard tracks, flattening every region towards the overall mix, and the spike adds the same Ambient listening everywhere. This illustrates that p[r,g] measures event-weighted demand, not latent preference (MATHEMATICS.md section 3).

## Interpretation limits

These results show that the implementation recovers patterns that were deliberately planted by a simple generator with known parameters; they do not show how real listeners behave. The playlist-add signal is omitted (no timestamped add events yet) and its weight is renormalized across the other signals. Precision@K with K = 3 is coarse. Synthetic users choose tracks independently given their taste, which is simpler than real social influence.
